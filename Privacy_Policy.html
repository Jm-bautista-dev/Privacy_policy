# PRIVACY POLICY ## Lambac Coffee Roastery Mobile Ordering and Customer Communication, and Business Analytics System **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026 **Last Updated:** November 12, 2026 ### 1. Introduction Lambac Coffee Roastery ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, Lambac Coffee Roastery Mobile Ordering and Customer Communication, and Business Analytics System ("the App"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our services. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. ### 2. Information We Collect Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information: **A. Account Information** - Full name - Email address - Account credentials and authentication information - User role and account status **B. Order Information** - Products selected and ordered - Order quantities and prices - Order dates and timestamps - Pickup and order status information - Order history **C. Communication Information** - Messages exchanged between customers and authorized staff - Customer inquiries and support requests - Reviews and feedback submitted through the App **D. Device and Technical Information** The App and its supporting services may process technical information necessary for authentication, security, notifications, and proper operation. This may include device or application identifiers, device information, and diagnostic information, depending on the services and features enabled. ### 3. How We Use Your Information We use collected information for the following purposes: - Creating and managing user accounts. - Authenticating users and maintaining account security. - Processing and managing customer pickup orders. - Displaying order status and order history. - Coordinating order preparation and pickup. - Allowing customers to communicate with authorized staff. - Sending account verification emails and relevant notifications. - Managing products, employees, and business operations. - Generating business analytics, sales reports, and forecasting information. - Improving application performance, reliability, and security. - Responding to customer inquiries and resolving technical issues. ### 4. Payment Information Lambac Coffee Roastery currently operates on a pickup-only ordering model. Customers pay at the store counter after picking up their orders. The App does not currently process online card payments or collect payment card numbers, banking credentials, or online payment account passwords. ### 5. Data Storage and Third-Party Services We use third-party services to support the operation of the App. **Google Firebase** The App uses Google Firebase services, including: - Firebase Authentication for account registration and login. - Cloud Firestore for storing application data, including account profiles, products, orders, and communications. Information processed through these services is subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices. **Email Verification Services** The App uses an email delivery service to send account verification codes (OTPs) to users during registration. Email addresses are processed for account verification and authentication purposes. We do not sell personal information to third parties. ### 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized personnel based on their assigned roles: - **Administrators:** May access information required for account management, business operations, reporting, and customer support. - **Baristas and authorized staff:** May access information necessary to manage and prepare customer orders and perform their assigned responsibilities. - **Customers:** May access their own account information, orders, and conversations. We may also disclose information when required by applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or legitimate security requirements. ### 7. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include authentication, role-based access controls, and the security features available through our cloud infrastructure. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ### 8. Data Retention We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain account records, fulfill operational requirements, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer necessary, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable retention requirements. ### 9.
